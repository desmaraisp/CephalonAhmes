**TRAIT LOCKING (LIVE)** 

One of the primary requests we’ve received for Riven Mods is to allow players to lock a stat to prevent it from being Cycled. Now, you can do so with a system called Trait Locking! 

One trait (stat) can be locked at a time while cycling a Riven, freezing it until the lock is removed or the Cycling menu is closed. 

There is no cost or item required to lock a trait. Locks can be removed and re-added for free! However, when cycling a Riven Mod with a locked trait, the Kuva cost is doubled. Locking a trait is entirely optional, and Rivens can still be cycled as usual without a lock. 

**How to Lock a Riven Trait:** 

**1.** Go to the Mod Segment in your Base of Operations 

**2.** Select the Riven Mod you’d like to cycle and select the “Cycle Riven” option 

**3.** Select the “Lock Trait” button 

[image.jpeg.297cbadb37d06a75880f99bcb778e](https://media.invisioncic.com/Mwarframe/monthly_2026_09/image.jpeg.297cbadb37d06a75880f99bcb778ea62.jpeg)

Image Description: Screenshot of the Riven Mod UI showing the Soma Argicron Mod. The options for Lock Trait and Cycle for 3,500 Kuva are shown and the cursor is hovering on Lock Trait. 

**4.** Select the trait you wish to lock. The locked trait will appear greyed out with a lock icon on the Mod and will also be listed above the “Lock Trait” button. 

[image.jpeg.698ee4c870076ab93d872770d0306](https://media.invisioncic.com/Mwarframe/monthly_2026_09/image.jpeg.698ee4c870076ab93d872770d03065b9.jpeg)

Image Description: Screenshot of the Choose A Trait To Lock UI screen. Two traits are displayed as options, the first is a 1.5 times Damage to Grineer, and the second is an increased Critical Chance by 167.2%. 

[image.jpeg.aeb65eb7d9f5a112b95f5d0dcdb30](https://media.invisioncic.com/Mwarframe/monthly_2026_09/image.jpeg.aeb65eb7d9f5a112b95f5d0dcdb305b6.jpeg)

Image Description: Screenshot of the Riven Mod UI screen showing the Soma Argircon Mod with a locked trait denoted by a lock icon. Above the Lock Trait option is the currently locked trait of increased Critical Chance by 167.2% and a remove icon next to it. 

**5**. Begin cycling your Riven with the locked trait! Notice that once locked, it will not change while cycling. 

[image.jpeg.9dd7ddb365946093af4cf8b6ad3b1](https://media.invisioncic.com/Mwarframe/monthly_2026_09/image.jpeg.9dd7ddb365946093af4cf8b6ad3b18ed.jpeg)

Image Description: Screenshot of the Riven Mod Cycling UI at the Cycle preview screen. We can see that the Riven Mod has a locked trait and the resulting cycled Mod retains the locked trait. 

  
It is important to note that a trait **needs to be re-locked each time you open this menu**, as they are not maintained once the menu has been closed. 

Cycling a Riven with a locked trait prevents the number of traits on the Mod from changing. This means a Mod with 3 positive traits and 1 negative trait will always cycle into another version with 3 positive traits and 1 negative trait if one of the traits is locked. To cycle into a different number of traits, unlock all traits and cycle again.

**BANSHEE REWORK** 

Banshee may not be at the top of your Loadout list, but those who have mastered her kit are able to deal a significant amount of damage in-mission. While not the flashiest pick, she can turn any weapon into something truly lethal — our goal is to maintain that lethality, while adding a few quality of life lifts to her Abilities. The following changes aim to make her kit feel fluid and impactful, while staying true to her identity as a sound-based Warframe. 

**Passive** 

Banshee’s Passive is helpful to a sect of players who value stealth, but largely has little effect in most missions. We are keeping her hush mechanic, but adding a little extra value that feels thematically appropriate for our sound-based Warframe. 

  * Banshee’s abilities pierce the ears of nearby enemies. Casting abilities applies maximum Puncture Status on foes within 10m. 
  * Banshee’s weapon noises are hushed. 



**First Ability - Sonic Boom** 

Sonic Boom is an excellent survival tool for Banshee, but its ragdoll mechanic does not synergize well with other elements of her kit. The following changes help improve its synergy, and improve Banshee’s overall survivability. 

  * Sonic Boom is now cast one-handed and no longer roots Banshee in place. 
  * Removed Ragdoll from affected enemies — now pushes them back and stuns them. 
    * The ragdoll did not synergize well with Sonar as it made it harder to target the weakpoints. 
  * Increased Impact damage from 50 to 250. 
  * Sonic Boom now reduces 70% of Armor, scaling with Ability Strength up to 100%. 
    * This mechanic was originally tied to Sonic Fracture, but is now part of her base kit. 
  * Sonic Boom now breaks resource containers and breakable decorations. 
  * Sonic Boom now deals a guaranteed Impact Status Effect. 
  * Renamed the “Strength” stat to “Push Force”, as it affects how strongly Sonic Boom will push enemies. 



**Sonic Fracture Augment** 

  * Renamed to “Sonic Siphon”. 
  * Banshee gains Armor for 20s if an enemy is hit by Sonic Siphon, up to a cap of 1500 Armor. 
    * Armor does not need to be stripped for this bonus Armor to apply! 
  * Recasting Sonic Siphon will refresh the Bonus Armor duration if an enemy is struck by the cast.

**Second Ability - Sonar** 

Sonar turns many weapons into a powerhouse in the right hands, but requires a lot of spam casting to reach its full potential. In addition, it offers little synergy with existing Weak Point enhancements as Sonar’s Weak Spots are considered a separate mechanic. These changes address common player feedback, while also giving the ability a light quality of life lift. 

  * Sonar’s Weak Spots are now considered Weak Points, giving them synergy with a variety of Mods, Arcanes, and more. 
  * Sonar now prioritizes unmarked parts of the body for its Weak Spots to help with Weak Spot legibility. 
    * The reticle now turns red when shooting at the Weak Spots, just like other Weak Points. 
  * Casting Sonar no longer roots Banshee in place. 
  * Updated SFX when hitting a Weak Spot. 



**Resonance Augment** 

  * Unchanged. 



**Third Ability - Silence** 

Silence is a powerful Ability, but its impact on the battlefield is not always clear to players. The following changes help improve the visibility of its effects, with a few quality of life touches! 

  * Silence can now be recast to reset its duration and reapply the Stun. 
  * Casting Silence no longer roots Banshee in place. 
  * Improved VFX on enemies to make it clearer they are affected by Silence. 



**Savage Silence Augment** 

  * Unchanged. 



**Fourth Ability - Sound Quake** 

Sound Quake is an iconic part of Banshee’s kit, but it struggles to synergize with her other Abilities. Our goal with the following changes is to make Sound Quake fit more fluidly into Banshee’s playstyle, while retaining the nostalgic elements that are emblematic of her kit. 

  * Sound Quake is no longer channelled. Casting the ability creates an area of effect with a duration of 25 seconds. 
  * Resonating Quake fans may be confused by this change — we are swapping the mechanics of the Augment and the base Ability to make Sound Quake synergize better with the rest of Banshee’s kit. 
  * Banshee is briefly invulnerable when casting Sound Quake. 
  * Enemies receive guaranteed Blast Status Effects while in Sound Quake’s area of effect, which reduces based on distance from the center. 
  * Increased casting animation speed to make it more fluid. 
  * Sound Quake now breaks containers within its entire area of effect, not just the 5 metres around the epicenter. 



**Resonating Quake Augment** 

  * Renamed to “Gaseous Quake”. 
  * Gaseous Quake is now a channelled Ability, which takes on the mechanic of Banshee’s old Sound Quake. 
  * Banshee is rooted in place to channel Gaseous Quake, which deals additional Gas Damage and Status Effects to enemies in the area of effect. 
  * Gaseous Quake’s damage is multiplied every second during cast, up to a cap. 
    * Energy drain also increases every second during casting. 
  * Gaseous Quake's area of effect now persists for a short duration after channelling ends. 
  * Improved Line of Sight now applies to Gaseous Quake. 
  * Updated VFX and SFX to reflect this new gassy styling of the Ability.

With this update we are changing the way that the vitiligo complexion, **Complexion B-17**, functions. Previously, the skin tone selected via Complexion Tint would apply across the entire skin and this would lead to unexpected tones or the erasure of vitiligo in some tone choices. 

Now Complexion Tint applies only to melanated areas which allows for a better tinting overall and greater respect for the tone selected. Through the addition of two new features we have expanded your ability to customize your skin tone with vitiligo. 

  * **Markings Coverage:** Lets you adjust the degree to which the vitiligo pattern is present (i.e. the dispersion of melanin) 
    * Note: In order for this option to become available, you must first select a complexion tint. 
  * **Invert Markings**: Lets you swap the current melanated vs lack of melanin regions within the vitiligo pattern

**QUALITY OF LIFE (QOL) CHANGES** 

**Weak Point Standardization** 

With our changes to Banshee’s Sonar, we are working on a wider standardization of Weak Points. There are a variety of Abilities and enhancements that trigger “On Headshot”, but enemies have a variety of weak points outside of just their heads (if they even have a head, depending on the enemy). As a result, we are working to convert all “On Headshot” triggers to be “On Weakpoint” instead. This is a large undertaking considering the vast array of Incarnons, Mods, Arcanes, Unique Traits, and more, so we’re prioritizing the most popular enhancements to start. 

Converted the following triggers from “On Headshot” to “On Weakpoint”: 

  * Longbow Sharpshot 
  * Argon Scope 
  * Hydraulic Crosshairs 
  * Galvanized Crosshairs 
  * Galvanized Scope 
  * Primary Deadhead 
  * Secondary Deadhead 
  * Arcane Precision 
  * Arcane Pistoleer 
  * Sharpshooter 
  * Harrow’s Thurible, Covenant and Lasting Covenant Augment 



In addition, we have converted Helios’ Detect Vulnerability Precept to apply Weak Points instead of weak spots so it matches Banshee’s updated Sonar. 

We’ll continue this effort in future updates!
